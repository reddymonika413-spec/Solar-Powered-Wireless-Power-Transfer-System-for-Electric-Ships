# Solar Powered Wireless Power Transfer System for Electric Ships

## Introduction

The increasing demand for clean and sustainable transportation has encouraged the adoption of electric ships in the marine industry. This project focuses on developing a Solar Powered Wireless Power Transfer System that enables efficient charging of electric ships using renewable solar energy and wireless power transfer technology.

## Project Overview

The proposed system combines solar energy generation with wireless charging to provide an eco-friendly and convenient charging solution for electric ships. Solar panels generate electrical energy, which is stored and transferred wirelessly through inductive coupling, eliminating the need for direct electrical connections.

## Objectives

- To utilize solar energy as a renewable source of power.
- To develop a wireless charging mechanism for electric ships.
- To reduce dependency on conventional charging methods.
- To improve safety and convenience during charging operations.
- To promote sustainable marine transportation.

## Features

- Renewable energy-based charging system.
- Wireless power transfer using inductive coupling.
- Environmentally friendly and energy efficient.
- Reduced cable maintenance and connection issues.
- Suitable for future smart port applications.

## Working Principle

Solar panels convert sunlight into electrical energy, which is stored in a battery system. The stored energy is then supplied to a wireless power transfer unit. Through inductive coupling, power is transferred from the transmitter coil to the receiver coil, enabling the charging of an electric ship without physical electrical contact.

## Applications

- Electric ships and boats
- Smart ports and docking stations
- Green marine transportation systems
- Sustainable energy solutions
